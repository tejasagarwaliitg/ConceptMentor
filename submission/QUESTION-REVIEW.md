## coordinates-A-ordered-pair

A point is 3 units to the right of the origin and 4 units above it. What are its coordinates?

1. $(3, 4)$
2. $(4, 3)$
3. $(-3, 4)$
4. $(3, -4)$

**Proposed answer:** $(3, 4)$
**Working:** Positive x points right and positive y points up, so x = 3 and y = 4. The ordered pair is (3, 4).
